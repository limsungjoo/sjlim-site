I build systems that make machine learning experiments reproducible, AI agents dependable, and compute infrastructure easier for researchers to use.

I care less about model scores than about where a score came from. Most of my work turns a judgement call into something a machine can check: a recording contract every run has to satisfy, a scheduler that sizes compute from the request instead of a dropdown, a boundary between what a repository declares and what a runner executes.

Earlier I worked on medical imaging, computer vision, segmentation, domain adaptation, and radiomics. I now work across MLOps and AI transformation, connecting model development, agent workflows, infrastructure, and the operational controls that hold them together.
